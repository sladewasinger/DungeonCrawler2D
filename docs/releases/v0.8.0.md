v0.8.0 makes combat clearer, adds more character to companions, and gives the
dungeon a more useful at-a-glance map.

## Added

- Choose between sword, dagger, and hammer combat styles, each with its own
  reach, pace, and impact.
- Tard the dino pet now farts occasionally, and Doux can chase his tail while
  waiting nearby.
- The HUD now includes a top-down minimap with nearby creatures, party members,
  and landmarks.

## Changed

- Attack previews follow you during the swing, keep the aim you chose, and stay
  out of sight while you recover.
- Shields give a clearer flash when they stop a hit, and well-timed swings can
  bat hostile spit back at its source.
- The minimap stays square as you resize the HUD, with cardinal directions that
  scale cleanly with the map.

## Removed

- No player-facing features were removed in this update.

## Fixed

- Attack depth, held-weapon flashes, and unarmed facing now read more reliably
  in the middle of a fight.
- Enemy and player hit areas better match what you see on screen.
- The Orc Warlord keeps pressure on the arena instead of losing his turn at the
  edge of a wall or shield.
- “I’m stuck” now finds a nearby safe platform without dropping you into a
  distant spawn area.
- After three minutes without gameplay activity, the game returns you to the
  title screen with a clear option to rejoin.

<!-- developer-only -->
- The admin portal uses a local shared-token boundary, a server-issued opaque
  continuation key in tab-scoped storage, peer-bound rate limits, and a
  bounded durable audit sink. `ADMIN_TOKEN` never appears in URLs or local
  storage; token rotation still requires a game-server restart.
- Authenticated admin observer updates replace manual player refreshes.
  Gameplay chat can also reach the capability-gated admin command service.
- Admin palette placement is server-authoritative, normalizes to tile centers,
  exposes definition metadata, and authorizes enemy/weapon right-click despawn.
- `GAMEPLAY_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MS` configures the server-authoritative gameplay idle
  lease. Neutral input heartbeats, pings, and admin-only connections do not
  refresh it; movement, aim changes, chat, and player actions do.
- Combat separates movement bodies, hurtboxes, attack volumes, guard colliders,
  and projectile volumes. Local and admin diagnostics can expose these plus
  line-of-sight and behavior state.
- The dino behavior architecture uses a shared scheduler and registry with
  per-dino server decision modules and client presentation modules. Its event
  sequence prevents one-shot presentation from retriggering every snapshot.
- Terraform defines retained DynamoDB operational history plus structured
  CloudWatch error monitoring. Applying and verifying those resources remains
  an operator deployment step.
