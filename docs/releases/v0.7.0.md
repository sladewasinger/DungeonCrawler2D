## Added

- Added authoritative compound ground effects for fire, water, oil, poison,
  smoke, and steam, including deterministic reactions, spread, fuel use, and
  source attribution.
- Added Pitchbloom and restored dedicated Plant Creeper artwork. Chort now uses
  a bounded directional flame attack, while existing enemy identities remain
  intact.
- Added connected puddles, poison bubbles, persistent fire fields, burning and
  oiled entity effects, and shared reduced-effects budgets for slower devices.
- Added one ballistic contract for thrown items so the preview, server arc,
  impact point, and landed object agree.

## Changed

- Burning entities can ignite oil beneath them, torches can ignite oiled
  targets, and burning oil remains visible until its finite fuel is consumed.
- Thrown torches land at the selected point, retain a grounded torch sprite and
  light, remain recoverable, and burn out when consumed by fire.
- Client prediction and reconciliation now use the same effective movement
  modifiers as the server, including application and expiry during replay.
- Outdoor terrain rendering now submits only visible cells from cached 32×32
  chunk plans, substantially reducing offscreen draw work on phones and slower
  laptops without changing world generation or runtime chunk size.
- Ordinary enemy populations refill around active players while respecting
  spawn safety, sanctuary, terrain, and encounter rules.

## Removed

- Removed the parallel legacy area implementation and the direction-only throw
  path from active Phaser gameplay.

## Fixed

- Fixed projectile and melee status attribution crashes caused by missing
  source or attacker context.
- Fixed elemental layers hiding actors, liquids drawing above fire, poison
  feedback disappearing, and fire emitters blinking when connected fields
  changed.
- Fixed short throws incorrectly traveling to maximum range and aligned landed
  torch visuals, light seeds, and pickup positions to the authoritative impact.
- Fixed slowed movement prediction jitter and stale modifier replay.
- Fixed ordinary enemy refill counting dead or special encounter enemies toward
  the live dungeon population.

<!-- developer-only -->
- Moved the legacy area implementation into the effects domain and added
  validated declarative area-reaction content with termination safeguards.
- Added bounded connected-region VFX rigs, pooled particle lifecycles, AOI-aware
  area replication, and deterministic elemental enemy fixtures.
- Preserved the production lighting renderer for this release. Experimental
  darkness and atmosphere work continues separately and is not part of v0.7.0.
