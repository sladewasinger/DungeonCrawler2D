## Added

- Dungeon districts now have distinct biome colors and generation styles, including open chambers, mazes, arenas, pools, and caverns.
- Safe rooms now have a friendly goblin food attendant who greets arriving crawlers by name.
- The opening screen now lets players choose their crawler's gender and character model.
- Deaths now leave persistent fallen bodies, directional splatter, and enemy-specific or character-specific gore fragments.
- Carnage intensity, blood visibility, splatter limits, and chunk limits can be configured from the game menu.
<!-- developer-only -->
- Biome identity is generated deterministically per district and shared by the 2D and 3D terrain presentation paths.
- NPC speech is replicated as a dedicated server event, with safe-room dialog and presentation isolated in feature-specific modules.
- Selected player skins are carried through the client/server protocol and authoritative entity snapshots.
- Death presentation consumes a dedicated captured event stream before entity pruning, while ordinary impact blood follows authoritative health snapshots.
- Ground blood, splatter streaks, sprite fragments, and corpses use elevation-aware projected depths and bounded client-side pools.
<!-- /developer-only -->

## Changed

- Dungeon geometry is now twice as large, creating wider corridors, larger rooms, and more space for multiplayer fights.
- Walking and sprinting are slower and more deliberate.
- Safe rooms, party rooms, and personal rooms now have distinct floor labels, door colors, ownership rules, and access restrictions.
- Party members share one party door in a safe room while retaining individual labeled doors inside their party room.
- Blood stains blend into the floor without dark circular outlines, while deaths use a much stronger carnage treatment.
<!-- developer-only -->
- World-generation feature coordinates, landmark links, height sampling, room pockets, and fixed fixtures now share the global two-times scale transform.
- Safe-room door snapshots are derived per viewer so personal and party ownership never leaks through shared world tiles.
- Organic and slime gore chunks crop the dying actor's real idle-frame pixels; skeletons retain the clearer bone-fragment presentation.
- The carnage and blood controls persist locally and are read at effect-spawn time without changing authoritative gameplay.
<!-- /developer-only -->

## Removed

- Removed duplicate safe-room doors for crawlers who belong to the same party.
- Removed placeholder triangle and circle gore chunks for organic enemies and slimes.
<!-- developer-only -->
- Removed death-effect dependence on the generic deferred visual-event queue.
- Removed flat, height-zero decal ordering from the 2D blood and corpse paths.
<!-- /developer-only -->

## Fixed

- Blood, gore, and fallen bodies now render in the actual 2D dungeon when players or enemies die.
- Ground blood now respects floor elevation and no longer draws over actors standing on a different height level.
- Fallen player and enemy bodies remain visible for one minute instead of disappearing with the live entity.
- Safe-room doors now enforce personal and party access consistently.
- Character selections now remain visible to other connected players.
<!-- developer-only -->
- Lethal snapshots preserve the target position, entity kind, definition, and player skin before authoritative pruning.
- Death VFX now resolve the latest plausible melee direction for directional ground spray and clear the matching pending swing.
- Corpse rendering resolves the correct idle animation frame for both content-defined monsters and selected player skins.
<!-- /developer-only -->
