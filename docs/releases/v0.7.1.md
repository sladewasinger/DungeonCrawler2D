title: Network and Visibility Refinements
---

## Added

- Added opt-in Experimental CorpNet smoothing for jitter-prone networks. It
  keeps the existing WebSocket connection, coalesces ordinary snapshots,
  preserves immediate critical updates, and holds stale movement safely during
  short delivery gaps. Standard networking remains the default.
- Added opt-in toon line-of-sight lighting with a bounded visibility mask.
  Its angular wall-shadow sweep and exact monotonic-elevation visibility rule
  cull hidden terrain before mesh submission instead of drawing a fully lit
  world under a black overlay. Classic lighting remains the default.
- Added compass markers for the nearest safe room and mini-boss arena.
- Added more frequent deterministic mini-boss variants, including a Greater
  Demon encounter with three Chorts.

## Changed

- Reduced terrain, entity, area-effect, and lighting work for constrained
  devices while preserving normal runtime chunk sizing and gameplay behavior.
  The constrained profile halves terrain retention, bounds offscreen entities
  and effects, and uses reduced area/status particle budgets. Weak laptops can
  force this profile from Advanced Settings → Graphics; automatic device
  selection remains the default.
- Prevented Toon-hidden remote damage, blood, and social one-shot effects from
  consuming presentation work while preserving local feedback and global
  flourishes.
- Smoothed Toon shadow boundaries with conservative diagonal contours while
  retaining tile-accurate terrain, entity, and effect visibility.
- Made enemy last-known-position searches wider and forward-biased.
- Bounded the floor-one spawn neighborhood to eight ordinary enemies, with no
  more than four of one archetype and a wider enemy-free handoff around the
  spawn-room exit. Enemy density elsewhere is unchanged.

## Removed

- Removed Greater Demons from ordinary roaming populations; they now appear as
  leaders of their authored mini-boss encounters.

## Fixed

- Fixed Pitchbloom oil projectiles so direct hits apply the oiled status while
  misses still resolve against terrain correctly.
- Eliminated movement hitches caused by compass landmark refreshes generating
  hundreds of offscreen chunks at once. Landmark lookup is now deterministic,
  bounded, and cached without populating the terrain cache.
- Removed overlapping additive lights from poison pools. Their puddles and
  bubbles remain visible without turning large poisoned areas neon green.
- Repaired versioned asset URLs and deployment-cache behavior so updated client
  assets do not remain hidden behind stale browser or service-worker caches.
- Prevented the initial nine-chunk outdoor activation from independently
  multiplying cohesive packs into an immediate spawn-room swarm.

<!-- developer-only -->
- Local development now binds both the browser client and authoritative server
  to the LAN by default, allowing phone testing against a WSL-hosted game.
<!-- /developer-only -->

Toon lighting and Experimental CorpNet smoothing are opt-in. Classic lighting
and standard networking remain the default modes. This document records release
metadata and intended behavior; it does not claim deployment or production
validation.
