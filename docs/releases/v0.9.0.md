version: 0.9.0
date: 2026-08-01
title: Ranged Combat and Enemy Pressure
---

v0.9.0 makes ranged fights less predictable and gives enemy groups more ways
to keep the pressure on.

## Added

- Ordinary ranged enemies can now release a second projectile as part of a
  bounded burst, with a short pause between releases.
- Enemy packs near the starting area now include both a Chort and a Pitchbloom.

## Changed

- Ranged projectiles now vary their landing point around the target, so repeated
  shots are less predictable.
- Up to five enemies can now pressure the same player at once when the fight
  calls for it.
- On desktop, pointing at an incoming ordinary spit within sword reach selects
  it for a return while keeping the visible swing aimed at the pointer.

## Removed

- No player-facing features were removed in this update.

## Fixed

- Blocking a hostile spit now follows its incoming path even when the launching
  enemy has moved, so a valid block is not lost because its owner changed
  position.
- Projectile contact now respects the player's actual vertical hurtbox, keeping
  elevated projectiles from registering outside the intended contact band.
- A ranged enemy cancels the remaining shot in a burst when its target is no
  longer a valid target.
