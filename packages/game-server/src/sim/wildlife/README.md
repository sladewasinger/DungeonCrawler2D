# Overworld Wildlife & Ecological POIs (`packages/game-server/src/sim/wildlife`)

Authoritative simulation of living forest wildlife, cognitive AI states, multi-faction ecology, and deterministic POI landmarks:
- **`creatureSpecs.ts`**: Archetype combat/sensory/territorial definitions (`wolf`, `bear`, `spider`, `boar`, `slime`, `goblin`, `orc`, `skeleton`, `golem`) and `WildlifeEntity` state contract (including `suspicion`, `stunnedTimer`, `howlTimer`, `targetKind`).
- **`scentTrail.ts`**: Timed scent-crumb buffer dropped by active or bleeding players outside Sanctuary.
- **`factionTargeting.ts` & `ecologyHelpers.ts`**: Dynamic multi-faction targeting across bandits (`goblin`/`orc`), ancient ruins (`skeleton`/`golem`), and distinct wildlife factions (`wolf`/`bear`/`boar`/`spider`/`slime`), keeping same-POI campmates allied while supporting wildlife-vs-enemy and wildlife-vs-wildlife combat, gradual `0..1` suspicion buildup/decay, buddy & corpse alerting, and ranged hit/bow wakeup hunting (`wakeEntityFromHit`).
- **`creatureBehaviors.ts` & `wolfPackAi.ts`**: Specialized steering, ally separation (`computeSeparation`), slime cohesion (`computeSlimeCohesion`), combat stand-off spacing, calm idle-pause patrol rhythms (`golem` holds stationary sentry idle), bear territorial bluffing vs meat-healing scavenging (`+25%` max HP), boar wall-crash `1.5s` stun (`boarCrash`, `1.35×` bonus damage taken), spider horizontal parabolic pounce, `0.65s` wolf howl telegraph (`wolfHowl`) -> `18`-tile rally, `<25%` HP lone-wolf flee, campfire wariness, and rear-flank dart bites.
- **`creatureNavigation.ts`**: Bounded A* pathfinding cache (`findTilePath`) with 1-tile platform jumping, tangential + radial `BlockingObstacle` avoidance (chests, campfires, dual-circle tents, props), `isClearSpot` spawn/patrol validation, velocity-aligned facing sync, and damped knockback velocity physics.
- **`creatureAi.ts`**: Shared 3-stage perception pipeline (`investigate` filling `?` -> timed `spottedPop` `!` -> locked-facing windups except Boar's pre-charge tracking), stealth visibility & noise radius scaling, outrun disengage for roaming enemies vs territorial chest-guard leashing for `orc` & `golem`, unreachable-ledge `2.5s` disengage, and Sanctuary safety.
- **`worldEcology.ts`, `lootManager.ts` & `projectileSim.ts`**: Validated POI guard & wildlife spawning, `emitNoise` for parabolic pebbles/combat/sprinting, `2.25×` silent stealth backstabs & `1.65×` bow ambush shots, tree-trunk-only arrow embedding (`OakTree`/`PineTree`/`Palisade`) while gliding over `Boulder`/`Stump` props, and manual-only `[E]`/tap recoverable arrow collection alongside pixel-art loot drops.
