# Modular Layered Character Renderer (`render/character/`)

Builds high-detail `32×32` (rendered at `0.75×` world scale for natural human proportions and future monster headroom), 1px-outlined 4-directional paper-doll sprites and held gear:

- **Directions, Stride & Airborne Tracks (`characterTypes.ts`, `characterSprites.ts`)**: Generates `south`, `north`, and `side` (mirrored East/West) variants with a 6-frame run cycle (leg stride, foot lift, counter-swinging arms, follow-through hair bounce), 4-frame breathing idle, 2-frame rising `jump`, and 2-frame descending `fall`. Because all body, armor, and hair layers are purely pose-driven (`CharacterPose`), future `crouch`/`layDown` postures simply add pose tables and automatically inherit every equipment piece, eye color, and hairstyle.
- **Ground-Anchored Shadow (`playerSprite.ts`)**: Draws the dynamic contact shadow as a separate ground-anchored `Graphics` ellipse beneath `actorGroup` so jumping lifts the hero cleanly above a shrinking ground shadow.
- **Layer Order (back to front)**: body/limbs/under-tunic + expressive `eyeColor` irises (`characterBodyLayers.ts`) -> legs/boots/chest/head armor (`characterArmorLayers.ts`) -> directional hair tucked cleanly beneath equipped hoods (`characterHairLayers.ts`).
- **Held Gear & Torch (`heldWeapons.ts`, `heldPlacement.ts`)**: Keeps main weapon consistently in the character's dominant right hand and off-hand torch (or left-hip lantern with 2-handed `bow`) in the left hand across all angles — melee/tools rest in a natural ~60° upward hero carry with stride sway, `bow` aims smoothly 360° (Y-flipped when aiming left), and rear gear Z-sorts behind the body.
- **Cache**: `characterSprites.ts` maintains a bounded 64-entry LRU cache (`south`/`north`/`side` + `previewUrl`), destroying evicted Pixi textures cleanly.
