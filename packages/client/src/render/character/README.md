# Modular Layered Character Renderer (`render/character/`)

Builds high-detail `32×32` (rendered at `0.75×` world scale for natural human proportions and future monster headroom), 1px-outlined 4-directional paper-doll sprites and held gear:

- **Directions, Stride & Airborne Tracks (`characterTypes.ts`, `characterSprites.ts`, `armRig.ts`)**: Generates armless in-world `south`, `north`, and `side` (mirrored East/West) body/armor/hair frames with a 6-frame run cycle (leg stride, foot lift, counter-swinging hips, follow-through hair bounce), 4-frame breathing idle, 2-frame rising `jump`, and 2-frame descending `fall`, paired with separate shoulder-pivoted `frontArm` and `backArm` textures (`buildArmRects`) plus a Hero Modal `previewUrl` rendered with resting arms. Future hand-drawn spritesheets only need one armless body sheet and sleeve/arm textures to automatically drive swords, axes, bows, lanterns, and torches across all angles.
- **Ground-Anchored Shadow (`playerSprite.ts`)**: Draws the dynamic contact shadow as a separate ground-anchored `Graphics` ellipse beneath `actorGroup` so jumping lifts the hero cleanly above a shrinking ground shadow.
- **Layer Order (back to front)**: body/chest/under-tunic (`male` broad flat chest vs `female` front bust + hourglass S-curve lower back/hip side silhouette) + expressive `eyeColor` irises (`characterBodyLayers.ts`) -> legs/boots/chest/head armor matching male/female contours (`characterArmorLayers.ts`) -> directional hair tucked cleanly beneath equipped hoods (`characterHairLayers.ts`), composited dynamically with `backSlot`/`frontSlot` articulated arms and held gear.
- **Weapon Grip Sockets, Shields, Combat Poses & Built-In Lantern (`heldWeapons.ts`, `heldPlacement.ts`, `armRig.ts`)**: Keeps main weapons in the dominant right hand and the glowing brass **Lantern** in the left hand (`"handLantern"` with gentle pendulum swing for 1-handed tools/empty-handed, `"hipLantern"` at left waist when drawing 2-handed weapons or equipping a shield, and `"none"` when holding a craftable `torch`). `getShieldTexture()` bakes the off-hand wooden buckler, `computeHeldLayout` drives 3-hit directional melee swing arcs plus raised shield blocking (`blocking: true`), weapons declare `WeaponGripSockets` (`primary` + optional `secondary` + `twoHandStyle: "bowDraw" | "shaftTwoHand"`), gripping hands match `armH` (`3×3` male / `2×2` female), and `computeArmRig` connects the left arm to equipped shields, pulls the bow draw hand smoothly with `bowCharge`, tucks cleanly on front/back midline crosses, and hangs free arms at `90°` at rest.
- **Cache**: `characterSprites.ts` maintains a bounded 64-entry LRU cache (`south`/`north`/`side` + `previewUrl`), destroying evicted Pixi textures cleanly.
