# Combat VFX, Target & Gore Renderer (`render/combat/`)

Client-side visual feedback layers driven by authoritative `@dc2d/engine` `WorldEvent`, `WorldEntitySnapshot`, and `ProjectileSnapshot` streams:

- **Tuning Config (`combatVisualTuning.ts`)**: Single editable config exposing `MELEE_SWING_TUNING` (swing timings, combo arc sweeps/reaches/lunge, progressive smear radii/colors, hitstop durations) and `GORE_TUNING` (decal pool cap, gravity, fresh/dried palettes for all 6 substances, splatter/streak/drip/chunk/explode physics).
- **Composable HD-Pixel Gore (`goreSystem.ts`, `goreDecals.ts`, `goreTextures.ts`, `gorePrefs.ts`)**: Pairs `GoreStyle` (`splatter`, `thin-streak`, `drip`, `chunk-spray`, `explode-away`) orthogonally with `GoreSubstance` (`blood`, `slime`, `ichor`, `straw`, `bone`, `stone`). Pre-bakes nearest-neighbor luminance masks (`goreTextures.ts`), launches weapon-sweep biased droplets/streaks and bouncing chunks (`goreSystem.ts`) that drop off cliff edges cleanly and stamp into terrain-congruent decals (`goreDecals.ts` — elevated above plateau strips, dispersing as drifting plumes in water, drying naturally from fresh to aged colors over `14s`, expanding corpse pools on liquid kills, leaving bloody/slimy footprints after stepping through fresh puddles, and scaling cleanly across Quality Presets and the `Full / Reduced / Off` UI toggle).
- **Target, Wildlife & Guard Sprites (`creatureDrawers.ts`, `entitySprites.ts`, `entityAnimator.ts`)**: Uses preloaded transparent pixel-art spritesheets from `/assets/creatures/` (falling back to procedural graphics for training dummies) for overworld wildlife (`wolf`, `bear`, `spider`, `boar`, `slime`) and POI guards (`goblin`, `orc`, `skeleton`, `golem`) with automatic 4-directional (`south`, `side`, `north`) or 8-directional (`south`, `se`, `side`, `ne`, `north` mirrored for W/SW/NW on `skeleton` & `boar`) octant selection, ID-hashed phase (`hashIdPhase`), actual-displacement movement gating, jump lift (`jumpZ`), windup arcs, hit wobble, stagger tint, damaged HP bars, and fallen corpses.
- **Chests & Ground Loot (`worldPickups.ts`)**: Renders Bandit Camp wooden and Ancient Ruins stone chests using preloaded `64×64` open/closed PNGs from `/assets/pois/` with an interactive `[E] Open Chest` / tap prompt when near, plus bobbing magnetized ground loot pickups.
- **Combat Juice (`combatVfx.ts`)**: Renders actor-tracked progressive crescent blade smears (`moveTo`-anchored outer rim + tapering fill), rotated charge-streaked arrows, hero overhead bow-draw charge bar, gold parry bursts, block sparks, floating damage/status popups (distinct vivid crimson when hitting the local player), and a `45–55ms` hitstop freeze feel on impactful hits and parries.
