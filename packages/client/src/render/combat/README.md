# Combat VFX, Target & Gore Renderer (`render/combat/`)

Client-side visual feedback layers driven by authoritative `@dc2d/engine` `WorldEvent`, `WorldEntitySnapshot`, and `ProjectileSnapshot` streams:

- **Tuning Config (`combatVisualTuning.ts`)**: Single editable config exposing `MELEE_SWING_TUNING` (swing timings, combo arc sweeps/reaches/lunge, progressive smear radii/colors, hitstop durations) and `GORE_TUNING` (decal pool cap, gravity, fresh/dried palettes for all 6 substances, fade/dry timers).
- **Composable HD-Pixel Gore (`gore/`)**: Pairs `GoreStyle` orthogonally with `GoreSubstance` (`blood`, `slime`, `ichor`, `straw`, `bone`, `stone`) for weapon-matched curved blood slice arcs, chunky slime goop & puddles, skeleton bone chips & death bone piles, golem ephemeral pebbles & emissive sparks & death rubble piles, fluttering straw wisps, diffuse water clouds, elevated decals, and bloody footprint tracking (see `gore/README.md`).
- **Target, Wildlife & Guard Sprites (`creatureDrawers.ts`, `entitySprites.ts`, `entityAnimator.ts`, `aiStateIndicators.ts`)**: Uses preloaded transparent pixel-art spritesheets from `/assets/creatures/` (falling back to procedural graphics for training dummies) for overworld wildlife (`wolf`, `bear`, `spider`, `boar`, `slime`) and POI guards (`goblin`, `orc`, `skeleton`, `golem`) with automatic 4- or 8-directional octant selection, ID-hashed phase, actual-displacement movement gating, jump lift, hit wobble, stagger tint, damaged HP bars, hidden corpse sprites for `skeleton`/`golem` piles, ground-sorted opaque corpses (`CORPSE_GROUND_Z_OFFSET`), and diegetic overhead AI tells (`!` alert/windup, `!!` charge, `?` investigate, `« »` flank, `•••` feed + bob, and `ambush` stealth alpha).
- **Chests & Ground Loot (`worldPickups.ts`)**: Renders Bandit Camp wooden and Ancient Ruins stone chests using preloaded `64×64` open/closed PNGs from `/assets/pois/` with an interactive `[E] Open Chest` / tap prompt when near, plus bobbing magnetized ground loot pickups.
- **Combat Juice (`combatVfx.ts`) & Hitbox Overlay (`hitboxDebugLayer.ts`)**: Renders actor-tracked progressive crescent blade smears (`moveTo`-anchored outer rim + tapering fill), rotated charge-streaked arrows, embedded arrows (`arrowEmbed`), stone ricochet bursts (`arrowRicochet`), hero overhead bow-draw charge bar, gold parry bursts, block sparks, floating damage/status popups (distinct vivid crimson when hitting the local player), `45–55ms` hitstop freeze feel on impactful hits/parries, and a localhost/`?debug=1` hitbox/hurtbox debug layer.

> **AI Indicators Status (v0.11.3)**: `aiStateIndicators.ts` is an initial pass with known bugs (state flicker, stuck/mismatched markers, and positioning/visibility edge cases across animated rigs) scheduled alongside the Phase 4B AI behavior overhaul.
