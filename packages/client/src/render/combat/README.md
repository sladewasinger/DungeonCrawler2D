# Combat VFX, Target & Gore Renderer (`render/combat/`)

Client-side visual feedback layers driven by authoritative `@dc2d/engine` `WorldEvent`, `WorldEntitySnapshot`, and `ProjectileSnapshot` streams:

- **Tuning Config (`combatVisualTuning.ts`)**: Single editable config exposing `MELEE_SWING_TUNING` (swing timings, combo arc sweeps/reaches/lunge, progressive smear radii/colors, hitstop durations) and `GORE_TUNING` (decal pool cap, gravity, fresh/dried palettes for all 6 substances, fade/dry timers).
- **Composable HD-Pixel Gore ([`gore/README.md`](./gore/README.md))**: Pairs `GoreStyle` orthogonally with `GoreSubstance` (`blood`, `slime`, `ichor`, `straw`, `bone`, `stone`) for weapon-matched curved blood slice arcs, chunky slime goop & puddles, skeleton bone chips & death bone piles, golem ephemeral pebbles & emissive sparks & death rubble piles, fluttering straw wisps, diffuse water clouds, elevated decals, and bloody footprint tracking.
- **Target, Wildlife & Guard Sprites (`creatureDrawers.ts`, `entitySprites.ts`, `entityAnimator.ts`, `aiStateIndicators.ts`)**: Uses preloaded transparent 8-direction (`south`, `se`, `side` flip, `ne`, `north`) pixel-art spritesheets from `/assets/creatures/` (falling back to procedural graphics for training dummies) for overworld wildlife (`wolf`, `bear`, `spider`, `boar`, `slime`) and POI guards (`goblin`, `orc`, `skeleton`, `golem`) with automatic octant selection, ID-hashed phase, actual-displacement movement gating, jump lift, lowered deep-water wading (`0.65×` walk playback) with rounded meniscus waterline clipping and ~80% transparent blue-tinted submerged lower body, burning orange flicker, hit wobble, stagger tint, damaged HP bars, hidden corpse sprites for `skeleton`/`golem` piles, ground-sorted opaque corpses (`CORPSE_GROUND_Z_OFFSET`), and diegetic AI indicators (bottom-to-top filling `?` suspicion meter, orbiting golden dizzy stars on stunned boars, radial howl/roar sound-wave arcs, `!` spotted pop, windup diamond & `!!` charge, `« »` flank, `•••` feed, golden `RIPOSTE` diamond, and behind-unaware golden Backstab Reticle).
- **Chests & Ground Loot (`worldPickups.ts`, `lootViewHelpers.ts`)**: Renders Bandit Camp wooden and Ancient Ruins stone chests using preloaded `64×64` open/closed WebP sprites from `/assets/pois/` (`chest-{wood,stone}-{open,closed}.webp`) with an interactive `[E] Open Chest` / tap prompt when near, angled recoverable arrows drawn via shared `drawUnifiedArrowGraphic` (`[E] Pick up Arrow` / world tap manual collection), and `32×32` pixel-art item icon drops (`getItemHeldTexture`) with crisp hi-DPI (`resolution: 2`) count badges.
- **Combat VFX (`combatVfx.ts`, `weapons/`, [`webs/README.md`](./webs/README.md)) & Hitbox Overlay (`hitboxDebugLayer.ts`)**: Renders per-weapon animations, progressive crescent arcs (boosted radius & gold edge on `heavy` strikes), forward thrust/lunge lances, flame jets, overhead pickaxe slam shockwaves + chip flecks, alternating bare-fist `punch` speed-lines and fist impact arcs (`drawPunchImpact`), directional impact clash flashes (`drawImpactClash`), dynamic spring-cloth spiderweb layers + wrapped player cocoon shells (`webs/webRenderer.ts`), spinning `"web"` orbs, rotated charge-streaked arrows via `drawUnifiedArrowGraphic`, arcing 2.5D pebbles with shadows, ricochets (`arrowRicochet`), pebble ripples, `COCOONED!`/`FREED!`, `GUARD BREAK!`, `RIPOSTE!`, `DOUSED`, `AMBUSH!` & `BACKSTAB!` clashes, green poison / orange burn DoT popups, hero bow charge bars, parry/block sparks, hi-DPI popups, event-driven `hitstopSec`, and a `?debug=1` hitbox overlay.
