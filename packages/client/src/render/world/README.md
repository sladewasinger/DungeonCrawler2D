# World Scene (`packages/client/src/render/world`)

PixiJS v8 world rendering layer:
- `tileBuilder.ts`: Bakes `512×512` lower ground & water textures with ambient-occlusion cliff shadows and rounded shoreline masks.
- `cliffStrips.ts`: Bakes Y-sorted 3/4 lifted plateau row strips (`10px` per tier) with bevel rims, vertical cliff strata, and sloped staircases for smooth player occlusion.
- [`player/`](./player/README.md) (`playerSprite.ts`, `playerPoseHelpers.ts`, and `playerHeldLayout.ts`): Render the 8-directional 3D Hero (`scale = 0.20`) using baked 3D arms & armor sleeves across all poses (`idle`, `walk`, `run`, `jump`, `fall`, `dodge`, `downed`, `swim`, `bow`, `crouch_idle`, `crouch_walk`, and 3-frame `attack` synced to windup/strike/overheadSlam) + per-frame `handSockets` for sword/dagger/spear/greatsword/axe/pickaxe/torch/shield and upright side-carried bow when not aiming, golden blade charge pulse during `heavyCharge`, 3-frame `bow` draw clip + `weapon-bow.webp` overlay + dashed aim guide when aiming, rounded meniscus waterline masks (`render/character/water/waterSubmergeRig.ts`), and `GaitClock` stride syncing.
- `chunkRenderer.ts` & `chunkProps.ts`: Stream `32×32` chunks, mount `3×` HD-pixel props (`HD_PROP_SCALE = 1 / 3`), animate campfires & torches, fade tree canopies overhead, and drive base-anchored `(0.5, 0.94)` lever-arc grass/wildflower wind gusts + player push bending.
- `worldScene.ts`, `sceneCamera.ts`, `sceneHelpers.ts`, and [`npcs/`](./npcs/README.md): Orchestrate Option A integer-aligned zoom stops, camera rig/follow, combat/slam `shakePx` micro-shake, Pickaxe `groundCrack` & crit sparks, `burnExtinguish` steam poofs, multi-pass WebGL2 shaders (`grassWindStrength = 0`), cast shadows, torch/burn/cover-leaf particles, `playerLeave` smoke clouds, stone clash sparks, multi-actor water ripples, and Sanctuary NPC cues.
