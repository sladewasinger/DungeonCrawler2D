# Combat Rules (`@dc2d/engine`)

Pure, deterministic combat math shared between authoritative simulation and client prediction:

- **Tuning Config (`COMBAT_TUNING` in `combatRules.ts`)**: Single shared config object driving sanctuary center/radius, player vitals/stamina regen, combat movement multipliers (`dodge`, `swing`, `block`, `bowDraw`), dodge timings, melee combo scaling, parry/block windows, bow speed/damage, and bleed duration.
- **Sanctuary (`isInSanctuary`, `isInWorldSanctuary`)**: Safe spawn Town Hub glade (`r <= 14` tiles around `(0.5, 0.5)`) and any world Sanctuary POI (`isSanctuary: true` + `1.5`-tile pad) preventing PvP damage and keeping hostile creatures out.
- **Bow & Shield (`evaluateBowShot`, `isWithinSector`, `computeMitigatedDamage`)**: Hold-to-charge arrow velocity/damage curve, directional attack/block sector hit-testing, dodge-roll stamina/i-frame timings, and defense mitigation.
- **Composable Gore (`GoreStyle` × `GoreSubstance`)**: Orthogonal separation of weapon impact patterns (`splatter`, `thin-streak`, `drip`, `chunk-spray`, `explode-away`) from target tissue palettes (`blood`, `slime`, `ichor`, `straw`, `bone`, `stone`).
- **Distance-Driven Gait Clock (`gaitClock.ts`)**: Shared distance-accumulating stride clock (`WALK_CYCLE_TILES = 2.2`, `RUN_CYCLE_TILES = 3.1`) emitting unified animation phase and heel-strike triggers (`phase = 0.25, 0.75`) for 1:1 ground-travel, sprite-frame, and footstep SFX synchronization.
- **Cognitive AI Sensing (`aiSensing.ts`)**: Pure `AI_TUNING` constants, `ENTITY_KINDS`, `AI_STATES`, `PROP_SPECS`-driven continuous elevation- & foliage-aware ray line of sight (`computeLineOfSightClarity`, `hasElevationLineOfSight`), charge-lane terrain/obstacle validation (`hasClearChargeLane`), visibility/noise-scaled vision-cone + hearing perception (`canHearTarget`, `canPerceiveTarget`), and wolf pack flank-ring slot math (`computeFlankRingSlot`).
- **Combat & Stealth Rules (`combatRules.ts`, `stealthRules.ts`)**: Single `COMBAT_TUNING` (`parryStaggerSec = 1.45`) and `STEALTH_TUNING` (`attackFlashSec = 0.6`, `attackFlashLightBoost = 0.28`) driving `PROP_SPECS`-backed cover/canopy/light exposure (`sampleStealthExposure`), crouch movement, pebble distraction, and `isBackstabAngle`.
- **Canonical Colliders, Hurtboxes & Hitboxes ([`hitboxes/README.md`](./hitboxes/README.md), `combatShapes.ts`, `hitboxOverlap.ts`)**: Shared engine-owned `GroundColliderShape` (8-way rotated/foreshortened `playerFootCollider` + `entityFootCollider`), elevation-aware padded `EllipseHurtbox` (`entityHurtbox`, `playerHurtbox`), and directional `AttackHitbox` (`buildEntityAttackHitbox`, `resolveEntityAttackHitbox`, `buildPlayerAttackHitbox`, `resolvePlayerAttackHitbox`, `attackHitboxHitsHurtbox`, `canReachMeleeTarget`, `projectileHitsHurtbox`) consumed identically by simulation, sandbox, and client debug rendering.
- **Weapon Movesets & Feel Tuning (`weapons/weaponMovesets.ts`, `weapons/weaponFeelTuning.ts`)**: Extensible `WEAPON_MOVESETS` + `MELEE_FEEL_TUNING` (resolving item archetypes dynamically via `@dc2d/content` `getItemDef`) defining 3-phase `windupEnd` / `activeEnd` timelines, `weightTier`, `hitstopSec`, `shakePx`, `knockbackMult`, `armorCrushMult`, `slamAoeRadius`, `ignites`, `twoHanded`, 2-step `axe` (`chop -> cleave`), heavy hold/guard-break constants, shield-poke, burn DoT, and harvest hooks across all 8 archetypes (`sword`, `axe`, `pickaxe`, `torch`, `unarmed`, `dagger`, `spear`, `greatsword`).
